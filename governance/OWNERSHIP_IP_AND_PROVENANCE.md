# Ownership, IP and Provenance Policy

## Product authority

The intended exclusive product owner and licensing authority for
ENGINEERING-WORKFLOW is **Eimy Herrer**.

Technical stewardship, review access or contribution credit does not itself
grant product ownership, distribution authority or commercial licensing rights.

## Current evidence status

The proprietary policy is active, but the exclusive-rights claim is not yet
accepted for every historical file.

The canonical machine-readable status is

## Contributions

Every contribution must have:

- a traceable human author or responsible human reviewer;
- a legitimate source;
- a file or scope classification;
- a rights basis;
- third-party licence identification where applicable;
- written exclusive licensing evidence when the contributor is not Eimy
  Herrer.

A contribution must not be merged merely because it is technically correct.

## Human collaborators

Existing references to Eimy Herrer and Johny as joint technical owners do not
prove joint copyright ownership and do not prove exclusive ownership by either
person.

Any copyrightable contribution not authored solely by Eimy Herrer requires a
written rights resolution or clean-room replacement before distribution.

## AI-assisted material

AI-assisted work requires evidence of meaningful human authorship and review.
The repository must distinguish:

- human-authored material;
- AI-assisted material with documented human creative control;
- unclassified AI output;
- third-party or imported material.

Unclassified AI-assisted material is not release-ready.

## Shared and third-party components

A shared or imported component must define:

- rights holder;
- source and provenance;
- licence and permitted use;
- modification and redistribution conditions;
- compatibility policy;
- exit or replacement path.

Open-source dependencies remain under their original licences. The proprietary
project notice applies only to material owned or exclusively controlled by the
project rights holder.

## Public repository warning

Public visibility is not an open-source licence. However, GitHub users receive
the platform rights necessary to view and fork public repositories, and
existing copies may remain after a later visibility change.

The repository should be made private before further confidential development
once the owner explicitly performs the separate repository-visibility action.
